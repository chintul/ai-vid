# Resource Optimization - Capacity Planning Best Practices

## Introduction

Resource Optimization forms a critical pillar of effective Capacity Planning, enabling organizations to maintain reliable, secure, and efficient IT operations. This guide provides comprehensive coverage of technical approaches, operational frameworks, and proven practices for excellence in this domain.

## Fundamentals

**Core concepts:**

**Key principles:**
- Reliability and availability
- Performance optimization
- Security and compliance
- Cost efficiency
- Scalability and flexibility

**Critical objectives:**
- Minimize downtime and disruptions
- Ensure data integrity and protection
- Maintain regulatory compliance
- Optimize resource utilization
- Enable business agility

**Success factors:**
- Technical expertise
- Process discipline
- Automation adoption
- Proactive monitoring
- Continuous improvement

## Disaster Recovery

**DR strategy:**

**Recovery objectives:**
- RPO (Recovery Point Objective): Data loss tolerance
- RTO (Recovery Time Objective): Downtime tolerance
- Tiering: Critical, important, routine
- Testing frequency: Quarterly minimum
- Documentation: Detailed procedures

**DR architecture:**
- Primary site: Production environment
- Secondary site: DR environment
- Data replication: Real-time or scheduled
- Failover mechanisms: Automated or manual
- Failback procedures: Planned return

**DR testing:**
- Tabletop exercises
- Partial failover tests
- Full DR drills
- Lessons learned
- Plan updates
