**— Anonymous**

### 2. "The path to reflection begins with a single step of intention."

### 9. "Choose reflection even when it's uncomfortable."
**— Wellness Expert**

The greatest growth in reflection happens outside comfort zones.

### 10. "Reflection flourishes in environments of intention and care."
**— Mindfulness Teacher**
