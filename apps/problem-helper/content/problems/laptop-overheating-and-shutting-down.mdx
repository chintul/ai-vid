# Laptop Overheating and Shutting Down: Complete Cooling Fix Guide

A laptop that overheats and shuts down unexpectedly is frustrating and potentially damaging to components. Modern laptops have built-in thermal protection that forces shutdown when temperatures reach critical levels—typically 90-100°C (194-212°F). This protects processors and other components from permanent heat damage. If your laptop frequently overheats, the cooling system isn't functioning properly. This guide identifies causes and provides proven solutions to restore safe operating temperatures.

## Conclusion

Laptop overheating typically results from dust-clogged vents, aging thermal paste, or environmental factors rather than component failure. Start with simple cleaning and environmental improvements before moving to component replacement or professional service.

Regular maintenance—cleaning vents quarterly, using on hard surfaces, managing ambient temperature—prevents most overheating issues from developing. When problems do occur, systematic troubleshooting usually identifies fixable causes.

If overheating persists despite cleaning and environmental improvements, consider professional thermal paste replacement or cooling system inspection. However, most users find that basic maintenance and improved usage habits restore normal operating temperatures and eliminate unexpected shutdowns.
